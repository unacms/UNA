# Regulatory notices

## Regulatory status (EU Cyber Resilience Act)

Akeeba Amazon S3 Connector is free and open-source software released under the GPLv3 license. It is developed and distributed on a purely non-commercial basis: there is no charge for the software or any version of it, no paid tier or edition, no bundled or gated services, and no plan to monetize it in the future. On this basis, it falls outside the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (the Cyber Resilience Act), which exempts free and open-source software supplied outside the course of a commercial activity. This statement reflects our assessment as of 26 October 2026 and will be revisited if the project's distribution model changes.
